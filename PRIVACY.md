# Privacy

OZLauncher processes all data locally on the device.

- No ads, analytics, telemetry, identifiers or crash uploads.
- No Internet, account, location, microphone, camera or broad storage permission.
- The app catalog is read only to display installed launchable apps.
- Wallpapers, custom icons and `.ozbackup` files are selected through Android's system document picker.
- Preferences and copied images remain in OZLauncher's private app storage.
- Removing OZLauncher through Android settings deletes its private data.

OZLauncher has no server and no privacy policy URL is required for runtime functionality.
